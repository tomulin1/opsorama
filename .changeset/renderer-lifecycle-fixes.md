"@opsorama/three": patch

Make renderer failures and lifecycle edges predictable. A node whose factory or `validate` throws is reported to `onError` once, with a message naming the node and the original error as `cause`, and stays an empty placeholder until it changes; the rest of the scene keeps rendering, its children keep their place and a sliced initial load still finishes and resolves `loaded`. An `attachment.anchor` the owner's model lacks is reported once per owner build and the node is placed at the owner's origin instead of breaking every later update. A graphics context lost before the first frame ends the load in the new final `SceneLoadPhase` `'error'` and rejects `loaded` with the reported error; the renderer does not resume after the browser restores the context, so hosts dispose it and mount a new one. A failed mount (a plugin setup or the first sync throws) removes only the canvas, label layer and indicator and releases the graphics context, keeping fallback HTML in the container. Labels are placed from the node poses of the frame being drawn instead of the previous frame. A mouse, pen or touch click on a label selects with origin `'pointer'` (`'keyboard'` only for keyboard activation), and label clicks, like canvas clicks, do nothing before the first frame. A selection is framed once instead of twice. A window resize no longer cancels a pan or pinch, the canvas pixel ratio follows browser zoom and moves to another screen, the brackets of a selected or hovered group follow its contents while they move, the hover mark clears over a pick target, and `getAnchor` updates only the anchor's world matrices. In the node preview, a lost pointer capture ends a drag (the preview no longer keeps drawing), and only the primary pointer turns the copy.
