Add shape, line and group nodes. `ShapeNode` (`kind: 'shape'`) draws a box, sphere or cylinder (`ShapeGeometry`: `BoxShape`, `SphereShape`, `CylinderShape`; one cylinder radius may be 0) standing on its position, with `color` (default: the palette role of its `state`, otherwise `accent`) and `opacity` 0–1. `LineNode` (`kind: 'line'`) draws a polyline through local `points`: a 1-pixel line for `width` 0, a lit tube that pointer events and selection hit for a `width` up to `MAX_LINE_WIDTH` (5 m), optionally `dashed`. `GroupNode` (`kind: 'group'`) is a container without geometry: nodes whose `parentId` names it move, turn and scale with it, `nodeView` reports it as always `'open'`, and `validateParents` accepts asset and group parents only. `route`, `controller` and `transfer` (`MotionFields`, with `MOTION_NODE_KINDS`, `MotionNode` and `isMotionNode`) now apply to assets, shapes and groups; `ControllerContext.node` and `ControllerFactory` take a `MotionNode`. `isContained` counts only asset containers, so groups keep the indoor shadow policy unchanged. `validateScene` and the scene JSON Schema cover the new kinds with the rules `shape`, `opacity`, `line-points` and `line-width`; messages for `parent-kind`, `parent-id`, `motion-owner` and `attachment-conflict` now name groups and shapes. The renderer builds every node kind in one place and needs no asset factory for the new kinds; every box, every sphere and every cylinder with the same radius ratio shares a unit geometry, so equal shapes of any size and color are instanced automatically, while transparent shapes never are. A click on a node that is not selectable selects its nearest selectable group ancestor (`selectionTarget`), and a marked group shows its corner brackets or glow around everything inside it (`GlowHighlight.show` takes `{ descendants: true }` for that). The React and Vue adapters add `Group`, `Shape` and `Line`; Vue node components gain the `shape` (object), `opacity`, `width` and `dashed` props.
