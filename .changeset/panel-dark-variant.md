A second color version of the panels: `data-panel-theme="dark"` on a panel or on any element around panels turns them into translucent navy cards with light text, brighter tones and a cyan accent, the colors of the action ring card. Variables set closer to the panels still win. List rows and selectable items now take the panel's text color even where the host styles every list item or button.
