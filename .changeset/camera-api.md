Add camera settings and a camera API. `SceneSettings.camera` (`CameraSettings`) selects the `projection` (`'orthographic'`, the default, or `'perspective'` with `fov` 20–75°), the pointer `controls` (`'pan'` or `'none'`), the overview direction (`yaw`, `pitch` in radians), the `overview` (`'ground'` or `'bounds'`) and `frameOnSelect`; the defaults reproduce the previous orthographic overview exactly, and replacing `camera` resets omitted fields to `DEFAULT_CAMERA_SETTINGS`. `SceneSettings.zoom` is the zoom of both projections. `createSceneStore` and `configure` take `SceneSettingsPatch`, where `camera` may be partial. Core also exports `CameraView`, `CameraTransition`, `CameraTransitionResult`, `DEFAULT_CAMERA_TRANSITION`, the camera ranges and enums and `validateCameraSettings`, `validateCameraView` and `validateCameraTransition`; `ZOOM_RANGE` moves into the camera module and is still exported. The renderer adds `getCameraView()`, `setCameraView(view, transition?)` and `onCameraChange(listener)`, and `frame(id, { fit, transition })` and `overview({ transition })` now return a promise of `'completed'` or `'interrupted'` (without a transition they behave as before). Transitions run on real time, are cuts with reduced motion, `motion: false` or before the first frame, and are interrupted by a drag, another move, clearing the selection or a zoom change from elsewhere. Panning works with both projections and labels behind the perspective camera are hidden. `createRendererSlot` lets the React and Vue `DashboardProvider` hold the renderer of their one `SceneCanvas`; both adapters add `useSceneRenderer()` and `useCameraView()`, and a second `SceneCanvas` under one provider reports an error that names the fix.
