Add mouse orbit, following a node and camera paths. `CameraSettings.controls` accepts `'orbit'`: a right-button drag or a Ctrl + primary-button drag turns the view around its target (yaw, and pitch within `CAMERA_PITCH_RANGE`), a primary drag still pans, and the context menu over the canvas is suppressed only while orbit is set. The renderer adds `followNode(id | null, { distance?, heading? })`, which keeps the target on a node's world position every frame (routes, controllers, transfers and carriers included; `heading: 'behind'` also turns the yaw, smoothed, behind the node and snaps with reduced motion) and resolves `'stopped'`, `'interrupted'` (a pan or another camera move; an orbit keeps following) or `'removed'`, plus `getFollowedNode()`; and `playCameraPath(path)`, which plays `CameraKeyframe`s (a view and/or a framed node, travel `seconds`, `hold`, `easing`, `loop`) on real seconds and returns `{ stop(), finished }` (`CameraPathPlayback`). Reduced motion and `motion: false` cut to each keyframe at its time; a drag, `frame`, `overview`, `setCameraView`, `followNode`, an outside zoom change or a new path interrupts a path. Core exports `CameraKeyframe`, `CameraPath`, `validateCameraPath` (errors name the JSON Pointer of the bad value and the fix), `CameraFollowOptions`, `CameraFollowHeading`, `CameraFollowEnd`, `validateCameraFollow` and the ranges `CAMERA_KEYFRAME_SECONDS`, `MAX_CAMERA_KEYFRAMES`, `CAMERA_FOLLOW_DISTANCE` and `CAMERA_FOLLOW_HEADINGS`. Camera validation messages list three or more choices as "a, b or c".
